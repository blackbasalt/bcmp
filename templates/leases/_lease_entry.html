{% comment %} Заведение аренды — форма в блоке аренды на карточке помещения. It stands where the помещение is already open in front of the reader and is submitted to the карточка's own address: no address is added by this stage, and a refusal has the карточка to come back onto (ADR 0005). The карточка arrives by `hx-get` into the rail of the экран этажа, so the answer to a write is the redrawn карточка going back into that same rail — there is no redirect anywhere here, and the screen is its own confirmation. This submission names nothing in `submitted`, and that is the rule the документ's page already follows: правка and удаление name themselves, and what does not name itself is the block's own form. `data-lease-form` is the block's contract, mirroring `data-upload` on the two upload forms: it says whether заведение is offered at all. A сотрудник without the administrator right does not get this markup — neither collapsed nor disabled: offered and refused at once reads as a breakage, and the карточка is read as a screen rather than as a бланк. The form is folded away, because a помещение is read far more often than an аренда is entered on it — and unfolded exactly where there is work for it: with a refusal at hand, because a reason hidden under a summary is a refusal nobody sees, and with a поиск under way, because the redrawn карточка must not swallow the question just asked. {% endcomment %}
