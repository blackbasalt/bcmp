It is called «выше» and «ниже» rather than "belongs to" and "contains": the `parent` relation means both nesting (cubicles in a lavatory) and the grouping of neighbouring spaces (a vestibule and the offices behind it), and the card must not claim which of the two is at hand. The tree neighbours are buttons, not links: they re-fill this same panel rather than leading away from the floor screen the panel exists for. They select a space by the same means as a tree node and a contour on the plan (`_space_select.html`). {% endcomment %}
