{% comment %} What a space is selected by — the screen's contract in one place, not four copies across three templates. It is substituted as attributes inside a tag: a tree node, a contour on the plan, and a tree neighbour within the card itself. The floor screen holds together precisely because the selection is the same everywhere — otherwise a click on a contour and a click on a node would diverge in behaviour. `data-select` is the space's code: it is how the tests find what selects it. `data-selected` says whether it is selected right now; both the node and the contour are highlighted by it. `hx-get` brings the card into the right-hand panel, and `selected` is an Alpine variable declared on the floor screen (`floor.html`): all of this works only inside it. {% endcomment %} data-select="{{ space.code }}" :data-selected="selected === '{{ space.pk }}' ? 'yes' : 'no'" hx-get="{% url 'building_passport:space_card' space.pk %}" hx-target="#space-card" x-on:click="selected = '{{ space.pk }}'"
